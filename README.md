Kabad Company


Building better connections between recyclable material supply and industrial demand.
Kabad Company is an early-stage project exploring how India's fragmented recycling and scrap ecosystem can become more connected, transparent, and efficient.
We are starting with a simple problem:
Recyclable material is available, and industrial buyers need it — but finding the right buyer, the right material, the right price, and the right logistics is often difficult.
Our initial focus is on plastic scrap and recycled materials, with early exploration centered around Delhi-NCR and surrounding industrial markets.

---

The Problem

India has a large and highly fragmented recycling ecosystem involving:
- Scrap dealers and aggregators
- Waste collectors
- Plastic recyclers
- Material processors
- Granule manufacturers
- Industrial manufacturers
- Logistics providers

However, information about material availability, specifications, pricing, quality, and buyer requirements is often fragmented across personal networks, phone calls, WhatsApp groups, and local relationships.

This creates several challenges:
- Buyers struggle to find consistent supply.
- Suppliers struggle to find suitable industrial buyers.
- Material prices can vary significantly by quality, location, and logistics.
- Quality and specifications are not always communicated clearly.
- Transportation can make an apparently profitable transaction unviable.
- Valuable transaction knowledge remains scattered instead of becoming usable data.

Kabad Company is exploring ways to solve these problems.

---

What We Are Building

We are not starting by building a large marketplace.
Instead, we are taking a field-first approach.
We are speaking directly with recyclers, processors, manufacturers, and scrap suppliers to understand:

1. What materials buyers actually need.
2. How much material they need.
3. What specifications and quality they require.
4. What prices work at the buyer's location.
5. Where suppliers are located.
6. What logistics costs look like.
7. Why potential transactions fail.
8. Which transactions can become recurring.

The first objective is to understand the economics of real transactions.
Technology comes after the problem is understood.

How the Initial Network Works

The initial workflow is intentionally simple:

Industrial Buyer

      ↓

Material Requirement

      ↓

Price + Quality + Quantity + Location

      ↓

Supplier Discovery

      ↓

Material Verification

      ↓

Logistics Check

      ↓

Transaction

      ↓

Transaction Data

      ↓

Learning & Repeat Supply


Over time, we want to understand whether this process can be improved through software, automation, data, and AI.

---


Why We Are Starting Manually


A common approach to marketplaces is to build software first and then search for users.


We are taking the opposite approach.


We want to:


Find the demand → understand the transaction → execute manually → collect data → identify repeatable patterns → then build technology.


This allows us to validate the business model before making large investments in software or infrastructure.
