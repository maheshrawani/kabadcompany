# Material Categories

## Kabad Company — Initial Material Intelligence Framework

**Status:** Early-stage research  
**Initial focus:** Recyclable plastics  
**Purpose:** Create a practical framework for understanding recyclable materials before attempting to automate sourcing or matching.

---

## 1. Why Material Classification Matters

A buyer requirement such as "need PP scrap" is usually not detailed enough to execute a commercial transaction.

The economic value of recyclable material can change based on:

- Polymer type
- Grade
- Form
- Color
- Source
- Cleanliness
- Moisture
- Contamination
- Processing history
- Quantity
- Location
- Transportation
- End-use application

Kabad Company therefore treats **material identification and specification** as an important part of the sourcing process.

The objective is not to create a theoretical material database. The objective is to understand which information is actually necessary for a buyer and supplier to complete a transaction.

---

## 2. Initial Plastic Categories

The initial research focuses on the following plastic families:

| Material | Common Name | Initial Research Interest |
|---|---|---|
| PP | Polypropylene | High-volume industrial plastic stream |
| HDPE | High-Density Polyethylene | Containers, packaging, industrial applications |
| LDPE | Low-Density Polyethylene | Films, bags, flexible packaging |
| PET | Polyethylene Terephthalate | Bottles, packaging, polyester-related applications |
| ABS | Acrylonitrile Butadiene Styrene | Engineering plastic and industrial applications |
| HIPS | High Impact Polystyrene | Industrial and consumer applications |
| Mixed Plastic | Mixed polymer streams | Requires stronger sorting/quality assessment |

These categories are a starting point, not a final list.

# 3. PP — Polypropylene

## Typical Sources

Potential sources include:

- Packaging
- Automotive components
- Household products
- Industrial components
- Woven sacks
- Crates
- Containers
- Production scrap

## Important Variables

For sourcing purposes, relevant variables may include:

- Rigid vs flexible
- Natural vs colored
- Homogeneous vs mixed
- Injection-grade vs other streams
- Regrind vs flakes vs other forms
- Contamination
- Moisture
- Quantity
- Location

## Questions for Buyers

- What PP grades do you accept?
- What forms are acceptable?
- Do you require washed material?
- What contamination limit is acceptable?
- Do you buy mixed colors?
- What monthly quantity is required?
- Is the requirement recurring?

# 9. Mixed Plastic

Mixed plastic is not a single material specification.

It may contain multiple polymer families, colors, forms, and contamination levels.

Therefore:

> "Mixed plastic" should trigger a qualification process rather than an automatic price.

Important questions include:

- Which polymers are present?
- Approximate percentage composition?
- Rigid or flexible?
- Washed or unwashed?
- Sorted or unsorted?
- Moisture?
- Dirt/contamination?
- Photos available?
- Sample available?
- Quantity?
- Location?
- Intended application?

Mixed material can sometimes have commercial value, but its economics depend strongly on the buyer and processing route.

---

# 10. Material Form

The physical form of material is often commercially important.

Possible forms include:

- Loose scrap
- Baled material
- Sorted scrap
- Flakes
- Washed flakes
- Regrind
- Reprocessed material
- Granules
- Compounded material

The same polymer family can have very different economics depending on its processing stage.

---

# 11. Quality Variables

Kabad Company's future sourcing records should capture quality information in a structured manner.

Potential fields include:

### Composition
What polymer or material is present?

### Color
Natural, transparent, white, black, mixed, or other.

### Contamination
Paper, metal, organic matter, other polymers, dirt, etc.

### Moisture
Important where washing or wet material is involved.

### Processing
Washed, shredded, ground, extruded, compounded, or unprocessed.

### Consistency
Whether the supplied material is reasonably consistent from load to load.

### Sample
Whether a physical sample has been inspected or approved.

---

# 12. Quantity

Quantity can affect commercial viability.

A buyer may have:

- One-time demand
- Trial quantity
- Partial truck requirement
- Full truck requirement
- Monthly recurring requirement
- Long-term contract requirement

A sourcing system should therefore record both:

**Available quantity**

and

**Expected recurring quantity**

because one truck does not necessarily represent a sustainable supply relationship.

---

# 13. Location

Location should be recorded at the operational level needed for freight estimation.

Useful information can include:

- City
- Industrial area
- Pickup region
- Buyer location
- Approximate distance
- Loading conditions

Exact private addresses should not be published in the public repository.

---

# 14. Price Basis

Price must be recorded together with its basis.

Examples:

- Ex-warehouse
- Ex-yard
- Pickup price
- Delivered price
- Delivered to buyer location
- Inclusive / exclusive of specific charges where relevant

A number without a price basis can be misleading.

For example:

> ₹X/kg

does not provide enough information to determine whether the deal is commercially viable.

A better record is:

> ₹X/kg delivered to buyer location, subject to agreed quality specifications.

---

# 15. Buyer-Specific Specifications

A major research principle is:

> **The buyer's specification is more useful than a generic material label.**

Two buyers may both purchase PP but require different:

- Grades
- Colors
- Processing levels
- Contamination limits
- Quantities
- Applications
- Payment terms

Therefore, the future system should preserve buyer-specific requirements instead of reducing everything to a single generic category.

---

Before presenting a supplier to a buyer, the following information should ideally be available:

```text
Material:
Grade:
Form:
Color:
Quantity:
Location:
Processing:
Quality:
Contamination:
Moisture:
Photos:
Sample:
Expected price:
Price basis:

Recurring supply:

```

Not every field will be required for every material.

The purpose of the checklist is to reduce avoidable uncertainty.

# 17. Future Data Structure

A future digital material record could look like:

```yaml
material:
  family: PP
  grade: buyer_specific
  form: regrind
  color: mixed
  quantity_mt: 20
  location:
    city: example_city
    region: NCR

quality:
  washed: false
  contamination: buyer_defined
  moisture: unknown

commercial:
  price: buyer_or_supplier_indication
  price_basis: delivered
  availability: immediate
  recurring: unknown

verification:
  photos: available
  sample: pending
  buyer_approved: false
```

This is an example data structure, not a standardized industry specification.

---

# 18. Why This Matters for AI

AI-based material matching should not be built around simple keyword matching.

For example:

```text
Buyer:
"Need 20 tonnes PP regrind in NCR."
```

A useful system should eventually understand that it needs to ask:

- What PP grade?
- What form?
- What color?
- What quality?
- What contamination tolerance?
- What price basis?
- What delivery timeline?
- Is this recurring?

Similarly, a supplier message such as:

```text
"PP ka 15 ton maal hai Ghaziabad mein."
```

contains only part of the information required for a reliable match.

The system should identify missing information rather than pretending the match is complete.

---

# 19. Research Priorities

Kabad Company will initially prioritize materials based on evidence from actual buyer requirements and completed or attempted transactions.

Questions to investigate:

1. Which materials have the strongest recurring demand?
2. Which materials have the largest price variation?
3. Which materials are easiest to verify remotely?
4. Which materials are most sensitive to freight?
5. Which materials have repeatable quality specifications?
6. Which materials can be sourced consistently?
7. Which material categories produce recurring buyer requirements?
8. Which categories are suitable for technology-assisted matching?

---

# 20. Important Principle

Material classification is not intended to create unnecessary complexity.

The objective is to collect **only the information that improves the probability of a successful transaction**.

The system should become more detailed where the market requires it and remain simple where it does not.
